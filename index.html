🎬 Cartoon Animation Agent

Free procedural 3D prototype · runs in your browser

Write a few short sentences. The agent splits them into scenes and chooses simple actions from the words in your script.

What this first version does
  • Creates randomized round-headed cartoon characters and outfits.
  • Builds a simple 3D outdoor scene with ground, trees and optional car.
  • Recognizes basic actions such as walk, run, sit, jump and look.
  • Plays scenes automatically with animated characters and camera movement.
  • Can record the preview as a WebM video where supported by your browser.

Honest limitation: This first engine is procedural 3D, not a generative AI model. It does not yet understand every story detail or generate photorealistic AI assets. It is a free foundation we can improve.
